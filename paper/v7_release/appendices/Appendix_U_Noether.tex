\section{Appendix U: Noether Currents and Conservation Laws (G0 Check)}
\label{app:noether}

The core Superfluid Lagrangian is invariant under global $U(1)$ phase rotation:
\begin{equation}
    \Phi(x) \to e^{i\alpha} \Phi(x) \quad \implies \quad \delta \mathcal{L} = 0
\end{equation}
By Noether's Theorem, this implies a conserved current $J^\mu$:
\begin{equation}
    J^\mu \equiv \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial (\partial_\mu \Phi)} \delta \Phi \approx \rho \partial^\mu \theta
\end{equation}
In the low-energy DFT limit, this corresponds to the conservation of particle number (or charge for charged superfluids). The associated continuity equation is:
\begin{equation}
    \nabla_\mu J^\mu = \nabla_\mu (\rho \partial^\mu \theta) = 0
\end{equation}
This derivation confirms that the hydrodynamic equations of motion used in the G3 simulation are consistent with the fundamental symmetries of the Lagrangian.
