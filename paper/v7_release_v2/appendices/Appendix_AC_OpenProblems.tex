\section{Appendix AC: The Open-Problem Register (2026)}
\label{app:AC}

Earlier versions declared the critical problems C1--C3 ``RESOLVED''. The adversarial campaign
reversed two of those stamps; this appendix is the honest register that replaces them. Each
item states what would close it.

\subsection*{AC.1 Reopened (formerly ``resolved'')}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Dark-sector coupling $\lambda_\chi$ from the Clifford tower.} The
representation-overlap and spectral-action estimates bracketed the value required by the
(now superseded) 5.7\,GeV relic story. With the tower sector at $184$--$201$\,TeV, the
coupling derivation must be redone for the new ground state. \emph{Closes when:} a
production/abundance computation for the tower ground state exists (phase-transition
production, per Section~\ref{sec:dark_matter_v12}'s mechanism).
\item \textbf{Sector assignments from $G_2$ branching.} Audit findings: the block selection
$3_0\oplus2_{+1}$ has no stated rule excluding the alternatives (the full $SU(2)$-charged
dimension is 7, not 5); sums of branching-block dimensions reach \emph{every} integer
$1$--$15$ (zero selective power); no map from representation blocks to winding classes is
defined; and $p_Z=8=\dim(\mathrm{adj}\,SU(3))$ assigns the colour-neutral $Z^0$ to the gluon
block. \emph{Closes when:} a winding-to-representation map is defined and the block rule is
fixed \emph{before} computing its dimension. (Note: the SM-exclusion theorem of
Section~\ref{sec:genesis-tower} makes this question moot for SM masses.)
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{AC.2 Genuinely open (current research programme)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Screening bookkeeping (GAP-S).} For $X=-(\partial\phi)^2/2<0$ (static sources)
and $c_4>0$, $P_X$ \emph{decreases}: $P_X$-enhancement k-mouflage cannot be the operative
mechanism under the declared sign conventions. The screening derivation must be redone with
explicit conventions; the working v17 gate uses an interpolation-function mechanism and is
unaffected.
\item \textbf{$\mathbb{CP}^2$-stage tower (GAP-N4b).} One-loop cascade matching at
$M_{\rm cond}$ (coefficient $b_{\mathbb{CP}^2}=3$) would fix the superheavy second tower.
\item \textbf{Independent audit of the $M^*$ chain (GAP-N4c).} The absolute tower scale
inherits the $v_F=1/5\to C=50/(3\pi)\to M^*=365.24$\,GeV chain (Appendix~VF); the lab has
not yet re-derived it independently.
\item \textbf{Tower phenomenology (GAP-N4d).} Relic abundance from the Great-Condensation
production, and the constraints on stable charged relics implied by the surviving-$U(1)$
theorem (a state charged under the electromagnetic $U(1)$ at $\sim\!200$\,TeV faces
anomalous-isotope bounds; a hidden-$U(1)$ assignment evades them --- this dichotomy is itself
falsifiable structure).
\item \textbf{Gradient-expansion remainder (GAP-N2)} --- the $(\partial^2\hat n)^2$
commutator class of the one-loop determinant; and \textbf{minimizer-constant refinement
(GAP-N3b)} --- per-$Q$ values of $\hat c$ from the Faddeev--Niemi tables.
\end{enumerate}

none is hidden in prose. This is the complete list --- a reader finding an open problem not
recorded here has found an error in this appendix.
