\subsection{U.6: Observable Constraint on $\beta$ from $\Delta m^2_{21}$}
\label{sec:mavan_constraint}
%% ============================================================

The correct experimental constraint on the MaVaN coupling $\beta$ follows from
requiring $\Delta m^2_{21}$ to be consistent between solar and long-baseline reactor
experiments.  This is a \emph{model-independent} calculation from primary oscillation
data.

\textbf{Derivation.} In MaVaN,
$\Delta m^2_{21}(\rho)=\Delta m^2_{21,0}\,(\rho/\rho_0)^{2\beta}$.
The ratio of neutrino production density (solar core,
$\rho_\odot\approx1.5\times10^5$~g~cm$^{-3}$) to the KamLAND detector density
($\rho_{\rm KamL}\approx4.5$~g~cm$^{-3}$, Earth mantle) gives:
\begin{equation}
    \frac{\Delta m^2_{21,\odot}}{\Delta m^2_{21,{\rm KamL}}}
    =\left(\frac{\rho_\odot}{\rho_{\rm KamL}}\right)^{2\beta}
    =\left(\frac{1.5\times10^5}{4.5}\right)^{2\beta}
    =\left(3.3\times10^4\right)^{2\beta}
\end{equation}

\textbf{Observed ratio} (NuFIT~5.0; Esteban et al.\ 2020, JHEP 2020:178):
$\Delta m^2_{21,\odot}/\Delta m^2_{21,{\rm KamL}}=7.50/7.53=0.996\pm0.056$~($2\sigma$).
\medskip

\textbf{Constraint:}
\begin{align}
    \left(3.3\times10^4\right)^{2\beta} &< 1.056\\
    2\beta\,\ln(3.3\times10^4) &< \ln(1.056)\\
    \beta &< \frac{\ln(1.056)}{2\,\ln(3.3\times10^4)}
             \approx \boxed{0.0018} \quad(2\sigma)
\end{align}

This implies the required stiffness at solar-core densities:
\begin{equation}
    n_{\rm micro} > \frac{2}{0.0018}-1 \approx 1110
\end{equation}
