5.1  Critical Unresolved Issues
Problem A — m_Phi = 8m Derivation [CRITICAL]
The Task 4 derivation m_Phi = 8m_source uses a circular self-consistency argument (assuming sigma ~ r_0 then solving for it). The result is falsified by NA64/LHC at any coupling >= 10^-13. Resolution: m_Phi must be treated as a Chameleon-running quantity (as in Part II), not a fixed prediction. A proper QFT treatment from first principles is needed.
Problem B — alpha_vac = 10^(-123): Why?
While the TRXT bridge reframes this as a topological question, it does not fully explain WHY (p_0,q_0) = (3,3,3) is the observed vacuum. The Diophantine constraint provides 3 candidates; the Chern-Simons action selects (3,3,3). But the question 'why is this the physical vacuum and not the ground-state energy minimum?' requires a deeper symmetry breaking argument. Three directions: (1) Anthropic/Landscape selection, (2) Kibble-Zurek mechanism at Planck epoch, (3) Euler characteristic as a hard topological constraint.
Problem C — Generation Mass Ratios
The mapping (3,3,3) <-> 1st gen, (2,4,4) <-> 2nd gen, (2,3,6) <-> 3rd gen is structurally motivated but the mass ratios do not match. Prediction: m_tau/m_mu ~ 1.447 vs observed 16.82. An additional Yukawa coupling mechanism is needed to generate the observed hierarchy from the CS action ratios.
Problem D — Decoherence Rate of Delta-Theta in Macroscopic Systems
For the screening mechanism to work correctly, the decoherence timescale of the phase mismatch field in laboratory environments must be much shorter than the interaction timescale. This has not been calculated explicitly. Without it, the screening claim remains [Unverified].
Problem E — Tensor T_uv Formulation
The naive form T_uv = Phi * g_uv (proposed in the original document) is equivalent to a cosmological constant and is insufficient. The full scalar-field canonical form used in Tasks 1-3 is correct but requires explicit derivation of the coupling between Phi_logic and the metric from the microscopic phase-mismatch action, not by analogy with quintessence.
Problem F — r_0 and M_* Calibration
Two constants remain to be fixed from observation: (1) M_* = 212.4 * E_Pl — testable only at Planck-scale physics; (2) the coefficients A, B of the running mass formula require fitting against CMB + DESI + Euclid void statistics. Task 8 should address this calibration pipeline.
